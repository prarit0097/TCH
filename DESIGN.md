# Circadian visual system

Official owner-supplied winged-horse logo, unchanged in colour and proportions. White and charcoal surfaces with restrained burgundy and gold accents complement the red/yellow mark. Self-hosted Manrope headings and body text keep information readable. Tokens are in src/styles/tokens.css.

The homepage introduces Dr BRC's operational Faridabad hospital through his portrait, location/capacity facts, DIP education and direct reception contact. Official educational photographs carry their actual Malaysia event captions; they never represent the Faridabad premises. Source links accompany educational material. Do not invent departments, qualifications, reviews, outcomes or opening hours.

UI/UX Pro Max research is recorded in docs/research/2026-11-04-ui-ux-redesign.md. Responsive layouts, light/dark themes, visible keyboard focus, 44px controls, reduced motion and enlarged-text navigation are checked in the browser. Keep the logo on white in both themes. Avoid looping decoration and autoplay.
