## Placement guidance

The clock photograph is the strongest authentic landscape hero candidate because the subject and circadian teaching aid are both clear. Preserve the visible clock in the crop; a 3:2 display or a shallow crop with object-position around 40% 50% works better than a tight portrait crop. The speaking image is a simpler alternative for biography or leadership content. The training image provides audience context.

These events took place in Malaysia. None of these photos shows the new Circadian Hospital in Faridabad, its premises, its clinical team or patients. Do not label them as such. Use a visible photo caption if the surrounding layout could otherwise imply hospital-premises photography. Keep supplied food art framed as DIP educational material, rather than evidence of local meal services or clinical results.

The official gallery also offers photos from the DIP Diet book launch in New Delhi (28 September 2024) and Circadian Doctor book launch in New Delhi (30 November 2023). These were reviewed but not selected: some include busy stage signage, many other people, or prominent medical-treatment slogans on clothing. No hospital-premises photo has been represented as Faridabad.

This research does not validate health or efficacy claims appearing on the source site. Image captions above are descriptive only.
